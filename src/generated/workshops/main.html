Who It's For

Built for the community, not around it.

These sessions are for whoever shows up: students figuring out their next step, shopkeepers wanting more customers, homemakers picking up a smartphone with confidence for the first time, and anyone curious about what AI actually means for them. No fee, no prerequisites, no assumption that you already know the basics.

The impact is meant to be immediate and local: a skill you can use the same week, a business that reaches a few more customers, a household that's a little less dependent on someone else to get online. Small, practical wins, in the community they're run in.
