What We Do

1

Competitions & Awards

Design challenges that invite students and young creators to solve real problems and imagine better futures. Winners get recognition, mentorship, and a platform to show their work.

2

Free Workshops for Rural Communities

Design-thinking workshops brought to small towns and villages at no cost, teaching problem-solving, teamwork and hands-on creative skills.

3

Archiving Local Design Knowledge

A public archive of India's local tools, crafts and everyday innovations, documented to preserve design wisdom and inspire future generations.

Our Mission

A platform that celebrates design excellence rooted in local wisdom, while fostering global collaboration.

We empower designers, preserve cultural heritage, and drive innovation through community-driven challenges and archives, work that starts local and is built to travel.

The Local Design Archive

A growing library of stories, crafts, tools, symbols, materials and everyday objects that shape local life. Each entry documents how design emerges naturally from culture and community: community tools and crafts, local innovations, photo and video documentation, oral histories and interviews, and student submissions and case studies.
