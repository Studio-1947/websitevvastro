A platform that celebrates design excellence rooted in local wisdom, while fostering global collaboration.

We empower designers, preserve cultural heritage, and drive innovation through community-driven challenges and archives, work that starts local and is built to travel.

For Artists & Craftspeople

Have a story worth telling?

If you're an artist, craftsperson or community keeping a local tradition alive, and you want your story documented and shared, we want to hear from you. Submissions become part of the Local Design Archive: photos, video, oral histories, the work itself.
