Where It Started

It began as the Himal Nagarik Fellowship.

The first cohort hired nine fellows from the hills around us. Three of them stayed on with Studio 1947 in full job roles once the fellowship ended, the clearest sign the model was working. It has grown since, but the idea hasn't changed: give local talent real exposure and a real stipend, not an unpaid internship.
