Not Charity. Conviction.

We’re a studio, not an NGO, but community capacity still matters to us.

Studio 1947 is a commercial design and technology studio, not a non-profit. We don’t run these initiatives as charity or as a CSR checkbox. We run them because the hills we work from only grow stronger when the people in them do too, and because some of our best collaborators started out as workshop participants.

01

Talent close to home.

Training local youth builds a pipeline of designers, developers and creators we can genuinely work with.

02

A stronger ecosystem.

The homestays and shops we support today become tomorrow’s case studies, clients and referrals.

03

Skills, not hand-outs.

We would rather teach a skill that compounds than offer help that runs out.

04

Technology for everyone.

Affordable, accessible technology shouldn’t be a privilege — that is why we run these workshops and build affordable solutions for local businesses.

A Founder’s Vision

“
I come from a farming family. Growing up, I saw first-hand how the lack of access to skills, information and confidence could hold an entire generation back, no matter how hard people worked. That is what capacity building means to me: not a buzzword, but the difference between facing life’s real challenges alone and having the tools to meet them. Studio 1947’s community work carries that belief forward.
Rabi (Rabiul Islam)
Founder & CEO, Studio 1947
