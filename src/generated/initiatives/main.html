Not Charity. Conviction.

We’re a studio, not an NGO, but community capacity still matters to us.

Studio 1947 is a commercial design and technology studio, not a non-profit. We don’t run these initiatives as charity or as a CSR checkbox. We run them because the hills we work from only grow stronger when the people in them do too, and because some of our best collaborators started out as workshop participants.

  • Talent close to home. Training local youth builds a pipeline of designers, developers and creators we can genuinely work with.
  • A stronger ecosystem. The homestays and shops we support today become tomorrow’s case studies, clients and referrals.
  • Skills, not hand-outs. We would rather teach a skill that compounds than offer help that runs out.
